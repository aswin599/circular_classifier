Abstract
In the modern digital era, having an engaging and responsive website
has become essential for every business, especially startups striving to build
their brand identity and reach a wider audience. Heal N Glow is a
wellness-based startup company focused on promoting natural beauty and
self-care through eco-friendly and organic skincare products. The company
recognized the need for a dedicated online platform that could effectively
represent its values, showcase its range of products, and connect with
potential customers. This necessity led to the development of the Heal N
Glow Website, a professional, user-friendly, and responsive web platform
designed to enhance the company’s digital presence and serve as a primary
channel of communication with its audience.
The Heal N Glow Website has been designed and implemented as a
frontend-only web application using modern web technologies such as
HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6), and React.js. React was chosen for its
component-based architecture, efficient rendering through the virtual DOM,
and scalability for future enhancements. The design focuses on aesthetics,
simplicity, and usability, ensuring a seamless browsing experience across
devices. Tools like Figma were used for interface design and prototyping,
while responsive design techniques ensure compatibility on mobile, tablet,
and desktop platforms. The website includes multiple sections—Home,
About, Products, Testimonials, and Contact—that together create a cohesive
and professional brand representation for Heal N Glow.
Deploying this project offers multiple advantages for the startup. It
provides a cost-effective and efficient solution for building brand visibility
and trust among customers without requiring complex backend systems. The
project also lays the foundation for future enhancements such as integrating a
backend with a database, enabling e-commerce functionalities like online
product ordering, user authentication, and content management. In the long
term, features such as SEO optimization, chat support, and analytics
integration can further improve customer engagement and marketing
performance. Thus, the “Heal N Glow Website” not only serves as a
present-day branding tool but also as a scalable framework for the
company’s future digital expansion

1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 INTRODUCTION
React.js
The core technology used in this project is React.js, a JavaScript library
developed by Facebook for building dynamic and reusable user interface components.
React simplifies the development of large web applications by allowing developers to
break the UI into independent, reusable pieces called components. It uses a Virtual DOM
to efficiently update only the necessary parts of a webpage, improving performance and
speed. React’s declarative nature makes the code more predictable and easier to debug,
while its component-based architecture enhances maintainability and scalability.
JavaScript (ES6)
JavaScript, particularly the ES6 (ECMAScript 2015) version, adds interactivity
and logical control to the website. ES6 introduces features like arrow functions, template
literals, and modular imports/exports that make the code more concise and maintainable.
JavaScript is used to handle user events, update the DOM dynamically, and integrate
different components of the website seamlessly, ensuring real-time responsiveness within
the client side.
GitHub for Collaboration
GitHub played a crucial role in version control and collaborative development
during the project. The development team utilized GitHub repositories to store the source
code, manage branches, and track changes. Using GitHub ensured a streamlined
workflow, reduced conflicts in collaborative environments, and allowed for continuous
integration and updates throughout the project lifecycle.
Vercel Platform as a Service (PaaS)
The completed website was hosted using Vercel, a modern cloud platform
designed for deploying frontend applications. Vercel offers a seamless integration with
GitHub, enabling automatic deployment whenever new changes are pushed to the main
branch. It provides fast global content delivery through its CDN (Content Delivery
Network), ensuring low latency and high availability. The simplicity of Vercel’s
deployment pipeline made it an ideal choice for this project, offering scalability,
reliability, and secure hosting without the need for complex configuration.

1.2 ORGANISATION PROFILE
Heal N Glow Pvt. Ltd. is a wellness and skincare startup established in 2024 in

beauty routines. The company emphasizes three core values—Purity, Sustainability, and
Transparency—which guide every stage of its product development and marketing

Heal N Glow offers a diverse range of skincare and self-care products, including
facial creams, gels, cleansers, face masks, lip balms, and wellness kits. All products are
formulated using plant-based ingredients and are free from harmful chemicals such as

premium-quality products that cater to the growing demand for clean and green beauty
solutions.
Heal N Glow’s strengths lie in its commitment to sustainability, product
authenticity, and customer satisfaction. The startup operates with a lean business model
that allows quick adaptation to changing market needs. By emphasizing eco-friendly
packaging, ethical sourcing, and transparency, the company aligns itself with global
standards of responsible beauty.
In the long term, Heal N Glow aims to expand its product line and introduce
advanced skincare solutions while retaining its core principles of natural wellness. The
company also plans to strengthen its digital ecosystem through improved website
features, customer relationship management tools, and data-driven marketing strategies.
In addition to retail and direct consumer sales, Heal N Glow seeks to establish
partnerships with hotels, resorts, and wellness centers by supplying premium skincare and
hospitality amenities tailored for the hospitality industry. With its strong foundation,
sustainable practices, and innovative approach, Heal N Glow continues to establish itself
as a promising and growing name in the Indian organic wellness sector.

2. PROBLEM DESCRIPTION
2.1 PROBLEM STATEMENT
In the current digital era, an effective online presence plays a crucial role in the
success of any business, especially for startups in the beauty and wellness industry.
Heal N Glow, a Dindigul-based Ayurvedic skincare brand, currently relies solely on
Instagram to showcase its products and engage with customers. While social media helps
reach audiences quickly, it lacks the structured presentation and functionality of a
dedicated website. As a result, customers face limitations in exploring the complete
product range, understanding their detailed benefits, or learning about the brand’s
philosophy and authenticity.
The absence of a centralized digital platform also restricts opportunities for
customer education, feedback collection, and sustained sales growth. Hence, there is a
need to develop a professional, user-friendly, and visually appealing website that can act
as a comprehensive digital storefront for Heal N Glow, reflecting the brand’s identity and
enhancing its market reach.
2.2 OBJECTIVES
● To design and develop a modern, responsive React-based website that effectively
represents the Heal N Glow brand.
● To showcase all products with detailed descriptions, usage instructions, and
benefits in a structured and user-friendly layout.
● To include sections for the brand story, customer testimonials, and contact
information to improve brand trust and engagement.
● To integrate visually rich components that align with the brand’s aesthetic values
and promote a premium feel.
● To deploy the project using Vercel (PaaS) for scalability and accessibility.
● To use GitHub for collaboration and version control, ensuring smooth team
coordination during development.

3. SYSTEM REQUIREMENT SPECIFICATION
3.1 SCOPE
This project focuses on developing a modern, responsive frontend website for
Heal N Glow, an Ayurvedic skincare startup based in Dindigul. The website serves as a
digital storefront, enabling the company to present its brand story, showcase its product
range, and engage customers effectively.

emphasizing aesthetics, usability, and brand identity. It is specifically designed for Heal N

screens.

3.2.1 SOFTWARE SPECIFICATION
Frontend Framework – React.js
The core of the Heal N Glow website is built using React.js, a JavaScript library
for building interactive and component-based user interfaces. React enables modular
development, allowing each section of the website, such as the Home, Products, About,
and Contact pages, to be created as reusable components. This ensures maintainability,
scalability, and efficient rendering of dynamic content through its virtual DOM
mechanism.
Styling – CSS
All visual styling is implemented using CSS, which allows precise control over
layout, typography, colors, and responsive design. CSS ensures that the website maintains
a consistent look and feel across desktop and mobile devices, aligning with the Heal N
Glow brand identity.
Animation Libraries – Framer Motion and Lenis Scroll
To enhance user experience, Framer Motion is employed for animations such as
fading, sliding, and interactive transitions, providing smooth visual feedback. Lenis
Scroll is integrated to deliver fluid scrolling effects, creating a seamless and modern
navigation experience throughout the website.
Development Environment – Visual Studio Code (VS Code)
The project is developed in Visual Studio Code, a lightweight and feature-rich IDE that
supports live previews, debugging, and integrated version control. VS Code provides a
flexible environment for writing React components, managing project files, and testing
code efficiently.
Version Control – Git & GitHub
Git is used to track code changes, while GitHub enables collaborative
development by allowing multiple contributors to work on different features
simultaneously. It ensures a structured workflow with version history, branch
management, and pull requests, making teamwork efficient and reducing code conflicts.
Deployment Platform – Vercel (PaaS)
The completed website is deployed using Vercel, a cloud-based Platform as a
Service. Vercel provides automatic builds and continuous deployment directly from the
GitHub repository. It also delivers website content through a global Content Delivery
Network (CDN), ensuring fast loading times and high availability for users worldwide.

4. PROPOSED SYSTEM
4.1 Methodology
The proposed system for Heal N Glow is a frontend-focused, responsive website
developed using React.js, with a strong emphasis on modular design, user experience, and
brand presentation. The development methodology combines component-based
architecture with the Agile development process, enabling iterative progress,
continuous feedback, and flexible enhancements. The methodology is detailed below by
feature:
1. Component-Based Architecture
Each section of the website Home, Products, About, Contact is developed as an
independent React component. This modular approach allows for easier maintenance,
testing, and future scalability. Components can be reused across the website, reducing
development time and ensuring consistency in layout and styling.
2. Responsive Design
The website is designed to work seamlessly across desktops, laptops, tablets, and
mobile devices. CSS is used in combination with modern layout techniques to adapt to
various screen sizes, ensuring that users have a consistent and intuitive experience on any
device.
3. Animations and Smooth Transitions
Visual interactivity is enhanced using Framer Motion for component animations
such as fading, sliding, and hover effects. Lenis Scroll is integrated for smooth scrolling
and navigation transitions, creating a polished, modern browsing experience. These
animations improve user engagement and make the website visually appealing without
compromising performance.
4. Agile Development Methodology
The project follows Agile methodology, incorporating iterative sprints to achieve
steady progress. Sprint planning included defining specific tasks for module creation, UI
design, animation integration, and responsiveness testing. Each sprint concluded with
internal reviews and testing, allowing early detection of errors, continuous improvement,
and flexibility for design changes.
5. Prototyping and Design Validation
Wireframes and mockups were created during the early stages of development to
visualize layouts, color schemes, and user interactions. These prototypes were validated
against the brand identity of Heal N Glow, ensuring that the final product aligned with
company goals and provided a cohesive user experience.

6. MODULE DESCRIPTION
6.1 Home Module
The Home module serves as the landing page of the website, providing
users with a comprehensive overview of the Heal N Glow brand. It showcases
featured products, brand highlights, and recent updates in a visually engaging
layout that immediately captures attention. This module integrates Framer
Motion animations and Lenis Scroll effects for smooth transitions between
sections, ensuring a modern, interactive user experience. Components include the
Hero Banner, Featured Products carousel, and Highlights section, all designed
to guide users seamlessly to other sections of the website.
6.2 About Us Module
The About Us module introduces the brand story, mission, values, and team
members, providing an in-depth understanding of the company’s philosophy. It
explains the Ayurvedic and organic principles behind the products, giving users
insight into the natural wellness approach adopted by the brand. Components
include Brand Story, Mission & Vision, and Team Introduction, with
animations and styling applied to enhance readability and visual appeal across
devices. This module also emphasizes the company’s commitment to quality and
sustainability, helping build credibility and trust with both individual customers
and institutional partners.

hotel management and hospitality partners. It provides a curated view of items
suitable for hotel amenities, with detailed descriptions and benefits. Components
include a Products Carousel, Testimonials, and Partners Section, enabling B2B
customers to quickly assess offerings and understand their relevance. Interactive
elements and smooth transitions ensure an engaging browsing experience for
professional users.
6.4 Hospitals Module
The Hospitals module highlights products suitable for healthcare facilities,
focusing on Ayurvedic wellness products that can be integrated into hospital and
wellness programs. Components include a Products Carousel, Testimonials, and
Partners Section, along with Product Cards that provide details, usage
instructions, and benefits. The layout is responsive and visually structured to help
healthcare professionals navigate quickly. This module also emphasizes product
safety, compliance with standards, and case studies where applicable, ensuring
users can trust the products for institutional use.
